# Country packs

- `list_country_packs`: The countries, registrations and base facts this service knows
- `list_obligation_definitions`: Every obligation in the library, with the source of each version
- `list_obligation_groups`: The obligations people name together, by country
- `list_schedules`: Amounts the law sets by bands of pay, by country and region
