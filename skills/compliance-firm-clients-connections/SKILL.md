# Firm clients connections

- `list_client_connections`: The business’s books and payroll connections
- `create_client_connection`: Connect the business’s books or payroll for it
- `list_client_connection_providers`: Where the business’s books and payroll can be read from
- `disconnect_client_connection`: Disconnect the business’s books
- `choose_client_connection_account`: Choose which account is the business’s
- `sync_client_connection`: Sync the business’s books now
