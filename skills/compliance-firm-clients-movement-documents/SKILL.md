# Firm clients movement documents

- `list_client_movement_documents`: The documents for goods moving kept for a client’s registration
- `read_client_carried_movement_documents`: Read the documents for goods assigned to a client’s registration to carry
- `read_client_carried_movement_documents_from`: Read the documents one business assigned to a client’s registration to carry
- `find_client_movement_document`: Find the document for goods moving a document of a client’s books has
- `read_client_movement_documents_of_day`: Read a client’s documents for goods moving the government holds for a day
- `look_up_for_client_movement_documents`: Look something up in the government's system for a client's goods movements
- `find_client_received_movement_document`: Find the document for goods coming to a client on a supplier's document at the government
- `match_client_received_movement_document`: The document for goods coming to a client on a supplier's document, as kept
- `get_client_movement_rules`: The rules for documents for goods moving under a client’s registration
- `list_client_movement_document_stops`: A client’s registrations whose documents for goods moving stop for missed returns
- `get_client_movement_document`: A client’s document for goods moving
- `refresh_client_movement_document`: Read a client’s document for goods moving again from the government
- `reject_client_movement_document`: Reject a document for goods coming in to a client
