# Firm clients registrations

- `list_client_registrations`: The business’s tax numbers its filings the firm does are owed under
- `add_client_registration`: Add a number a client is registered under
- `read_client_government_ledger`: Read a client's ledger entries from the government
- `read_client_government_notice`: Read a client's notice from the government whole
- `refresh_client_government_records`: Read the government's records of a client's registration afresh
- `get_client_government_return_regeneration`: Get the latest regeneration of a client's statement
- `regenerate_client_government_return`: Have the government make a client's statement again
- `check_client_government_return_regeneration`: Check a client's regeneration at the government
- `read_client_government_return_section`: Read a month of a client's return from the government
- `list_client_government_returns`: List a client's returns that can be read at the government
- `add_client_officer`: Add a person in a role at a client’s registration
- `change_client_officer`: Change a person entered by hand at a client’s registration
- `remove_client_officer`: Remove a person entered by hand at a client’s registration
