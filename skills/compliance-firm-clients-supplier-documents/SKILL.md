# Firm clients supplier documents

- `list_client_supplier_documents`: A client's suppliers' documents as last read
- `get_client_added_back_documents`: Rejected documents added back to a client's tax, as kept
- `read_client_added_back_documents`: Read rejected documents added back to a client's tax
- `count_client_supplier_documents`: Count a client's suppliers' documents at the government
- `get_client_supplier_document_coverage`: What the kept listings of a client's suppliers' documents cover
- `get_client_customer_responses`: Customers' responses to a client's documents as kept
- `read_client_customer_responses`: Read customers' responses to a client's documents
- `read_client_supplier_documents`: Read a client's suppliers' documents from the government
- `list_client_supplier_response_sets`: Responses sent for a client's suppliers' documents
- `respond_to_client_supplier_documents`: Respond to a client's suppliers' documents
- `get_client_supplier_response_set`: A set of responses to a client's suppliers' documents
- `check_client_supplier_response_set`: Check responses to a client's suppliers' documents at the government
- `get_client_supplier_document_rules`: The rules for responding to suppliers' documents under a client's registration
- `list_client_supplier_registrations`: A client's suppliers as their register last verified them
- `check_client_supplier_registrations`: Verify a client's suppliers due a check
- `withdraw_client_supplier_responses`: Withdraw responses to a client's suppliers' documents
