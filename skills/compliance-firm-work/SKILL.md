# Firm work

- `list_firm_work`: Every client’s filings the firm does, in one list
- `assign_firm_work`: Give filings to a person of the firm
- `get_firm_work_grid`: Clients by filings, for a period
- `get_firm_workload`: The firm’s workload, by person and by client
