# Government data

- `list_government_records`: The government's records of every registration, as kept
- `search_other_registrations`: Look for the registrations the government holds under the same holders
- `list_officers`: The people in roles at the registrations
- `set_filing_frequency`: Set how often the registration files, on its government account
- `read_government_ledger`: Read a ledger's entries from the government
- `read_government_notice`: Read a notice from the government whole
- `get_government_records`: The government's records of a registration, as kept
- `refresh_government_records`: Read the government's records of a registration afresh
- `get_government_return_regeneration`: Get the latest regeneration of a statement
- `regenerate_government_return`: Have the government make a statement again
- `check_government_return_regeneration`: Check a regeneration at the government
- `read_government_return_section`: Read a month of a return from the government
- `list_government_returns`: List the returns that can be read at the government
- `add_officer`: Add a person in a role at a registration
- `change_officer`: Change a person entered by hand
- `remove_officer`: Remove a person entered by hand
