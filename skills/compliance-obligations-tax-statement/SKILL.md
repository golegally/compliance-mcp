# Obligations tax statement

- `get_tax_statement`: A return’s tax statement
- `remove_tax_statement_document`: Remove a tax statement read
- `include_tax_statement_entry`: Count an unconfirmed entry of the tax statement
- `send_tax_statement_file`: Send the tax statement as its file
- `add_tax_payment`: Add a tax payment to the tax statement
- `remove_tax_payment`: Remove a tax payment from the tax statement
