# Reported documents

- `report_document`: Report a document to the government as it is issued
- `find_reported_document`: Find a document of the books at the government
- `look_up_reported_party`: Look up a business at the government's reporting system
- `get_document_reporting`: Whether a registration reports its documents as it issues them
- `get_reported_document`: A document reported to the government
- `cancel_reported_document`: Cancel a document reported to the government
- `issue_movement_document_with_report`: Issue the document for goods moving with a reported document
- `find_movement_document_of_report`: Find the document for goods moving a reported document has
- `get_printed_reported_document`: The printed reported document
- `read_reported_record`: Read a reported document from the portal's records
- `list_reported_lists`: A month's lists of reported documents asked for
- `start_reported_list`: Ask the portal for a month's list of reported documents
- `get_reported_list`: A list of reported documents asked for
- `check_reported_list`: Check a list of reported documents at the portal
- `get_reported_list_file`: A list's file
- `read_reported_summary`: Read a month's summary of reported documents from the portal
