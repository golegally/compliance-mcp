# Supplier documents

- `list_supplier_documents`: Suppliers' documents as last read
- `get_added_back_documents`: Rejected documents added back to the business’s tax, as kept
- `read_added_back_documents`: Read rejected documents added back to the business’s tax
- `count_supplier_documents`: Count suppliers' documents at the government
- `get_supplier_document_coverage`: What the kept listings of suppliers' documents cover
- `get_customer_responses`: Customers' responses to the business's documents as kept
- `read_customer_responses`: Read customers' responses to the business's documents
- `read_supplier_documents`: Read suppliers' documents from the government
- `list_supplier_response_sets`: Responses sent for suppliers' documents
- `respond_to_supplier_documents`: Respond to suppliers' documents
- `get_supplier_response_set`: A set of responses to suppliers' documents
- `check_supplier_response_set`: Check responses to suppliers' documents at the government
- `get_supplier_document_rules`: The rules for responding to suppliers' documents under a registration
- `list_supplier_registrations`: The registration's suppliers as their register last verified them
- `check_supplier_registrations`: Verify the registration's suppliers due a check
- `withdraw_supplier_responses`: Withdraw responses to suppliers' documents
